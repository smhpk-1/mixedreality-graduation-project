# Storyboard: Mixed Reality Graduation Project

## Scene 1: The Shift (Factory Setting)

### Step 1: The Awakening
**Visual:** The user opens their eyes to find themselves standing at a workstation in a dim, oppressive factory. A conveyor belt stretches out before them, humming with mechanical life. From the darkness above, a steady stream of red and blue cubes begins to descend onto the belt, moving relentlessly towards the user.
**Action:** The user looks around, establishing their presence in this industrial environment.
**Audio:** The low, rhythmic hum of machinery and the clanking of the conveyor belt fill the air, setting a monotonous tone.

### Step 2: The Error
**Visual:** The user reaches out with a virtual hand and grabs a red cube from the moving belt. They attempt to place it into a bin clearly marked for blue items.
**Action:** As the red cube nears the blue bin, the system rejects the action.
**Audio:** A comical, jarring "buzzer" or "error" sound plays, signaling a mistake and reinforcing the rigid rules of the factory.

### Step 3: The Success
**Visual:** Correcting their action, the user grabs a blue cube and guides it towards the matching blue bin. The cube slides smoothly into the receptacle without resistance.
**Action:** The user successfully sorts the object according to the factory's demands.
**Audio:** A satisfying, crisp "ding" or chime sounds, providing positive reinforcement and a brief sense of accomplishment in the repetitive task.

### Step 4: The Anomaly
**Visual:** The routine is suddenly broken. The red and blue cubes stop falling. Instead, glowing, bright green cubes—unlike anything seen before—appear on the belt, pulsing with an otherworldly light.
**Action:** As the user focuses on these anomalies or reaches out to touch one, the factory walls begin to dissolve.
**Audio:** The mechanical hum distorts into a surreal, rising tone. The visual reality warps, transitioning the user immediately into the next scene.

## Scene 2: The Playground (Creative Space)

### Step 1: The New World
**Visual:** The user spawns in a completely different space: a vast, empty room with neutral white walls, bathed in bright, soft light. Scattered across the floor is a grid of vibrant, colorful cubes (red, blue, green, yellow, purple), contrasting sharply with the previous gloom.
**Action:** The user looks down at the colorful array, realizing the freedom and lack of machinery in this new space.
**Audio:** The oppressive factory noise is gone, replaced by a serene, dreamlike silence or a gentle, magical ambience.

### Step 2: Exploration
**Visual:** The user steps forward and picks up a red cube from the ground. They hold it up to the light, examining its vivid color and unique properties.
**Action:** The user rotates the cube in their hand, inspecting it closely and realizing it is not a product to be sorted, but an object to be played with.
**Audio:** A subtle, pleasant hum or chime emanates from the cube when held, inviting interaction.

### Step 3: Creation
**Visual:** The user throws the red cube across the room. It arcs through the air and strikes a white wall. Upon impact, the wall instantly changes color, becoming a massive canvas of that same vibrant red.
**Action:** The user discovers their power to paint the world through play.
**Audio:** A satisfying, musical impact sound echoes as the wall transforms, emphasizing the joy of the action.

### Step 4: The Masterpiece
**Visual:** The scene concludes with a general view of the room, which has been completely transformed. The once-empty white space is now a chaotic, beautiful mosaic of colors. Walls are splashed with red, blue, green, and purple, standing as a testament to the user's creative expression.
**Action:** The user stands in the center of their creation, taking in the vibrant environment they have shaped.
**Audio:** A triumphant or peaceful musical swell concludes the experience, celebrating the freedom of the playground.
